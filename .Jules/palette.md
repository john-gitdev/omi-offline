## 2026-04-16 - Adding Tooltips to IconButtons\n**Learning:** In Flutter, `IconButton` widgets used for icon-only actions (like back buttons or refresh buttons) often lack accessibility labels by default. Adding a `tooltip` property is a simple, low-risk way to expose these labels to screen readers and improve the hover experience for mouse users without changing the visual layout.\n**Action:** Whenever introducing or modifying `IconButton` widgets, ensure the `tooltip` property is explicitly set to provide a semantic accessibility label.
